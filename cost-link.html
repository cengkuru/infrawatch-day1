What CoST adds · the publication grammar

CoST is not just the data standard. It is what makes the value test runnable.

InfraWatch asks whether a foreign-funded project was worth it. That question cannot be answered from the outside unless specific records are published first. The CoST Infrastructure Data Standard names those records. Without the standard, every InfraWatch question stalls at a sealed file.

The claim, stated plainly. CIPE owns the InfraWatch method and its comparative scoring. IEA-Kenya owns the net-present-value test. CoST supplies the one thing both depend on: a shared, published list of fields that turns a hidden project into an assessable one. Move the standard, and the method has nothing to run on. This is why "CoST = the data standard" undersells it. The standard is the precondition, not a side input.

Each InfraWatch question maps to a field that has to be published

Read across: the question InfraWatch asks, the CoST IDS field that answers it, and what stays unknowable if that field is never published. The third column is the cost of the standard not being run.

Move InfraWatch asks CoST IDS field that answers it Without it, you cannot know
See it Who is actually funding this, and on what terms? Funding source · financing terms whether the debt, not the asset, is the real story
See it Who really gains from the project? Beneficial ownership · offtake whether public money is routed to a private interest
See it What will this commit the state to, long term? Contract · fiscal commitments the size of the bill that lands years after the ribbon-cutting
Test it Was the cost honest, or marked up? Itemised cost · appraisal whether the price reflects the work or a buried margin
Test it Can the project's value be judged at all? Published terms (the precondition for the NPV test) anything: a withheld contract is a wall against assessment
Test it Did it perform as promised? Implementation · performance whether delivery matched the deal that was signed
Voice it Can the affected public challenge the terms? Contract disclosure reaching funder + contractor the public has no standing, because it has no record to point to

Three legs, three owners, one dependency

InfraWatch runs on three method legs. Two are owned by others. All three sit on top of the CoST publication layer.

The method & scoring

owned by CIPE / Verité

The comparative case design and the sequential scoring scale that rank disclosure across projects and countries.

The value test

owned by IEA-Kenya

The net-present-value and political-economy work that turns published terms into a verdict on whether a project was worth it.

The publication grammar

CoST IDS

The shared list of fields that has to be published before either of the other two legs has anything to work on. The precondition the whole method rests on.
