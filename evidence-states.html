Evidence states · the label before every score

Before you score zero, answer one question: why is it missing?

An empty field is not automatically a zero. Classify why it is empty first: only a point that applies, is already due, and was sufficiently searched with nothing found scores 0 and counts in the project result. Everything else gets a label, not a score.

How demanding a real zero is

The contract-variation reason, from the report's worked examples.

  • Official evidence shows a variation occurred, so the point applies.
  • The variation is in effect, so the reason is already due.
  • A sufficient search of the government channels is complete and recorded.
  • No published reason found.
  • Only now: 0-D, with the search record retained as its evidence.
The common trap: treating every empty cell as a finding. The DR Congo case study is pre-construction, so its completion documents cannot exist yet; its zeros were never disclosure failures. Only 0-D lowers the project result.
