How to read the status. figures verified means the underlying dataset is fixed and checked (the ten-project set, n=10). figures to confirm means the pattern holds but exact field labels should be confirmed against the CoST IDS v2.0 and figures against the loan agreements before external use. working read / teaching probe means it is a read of the room or a conceptual argument from the workshop, recorded as heard, to be confirmed with each member or against a clean source. InfraWatch Nairobi findings ledger, compiled from the visual essays of the workshop of 2 to 4 June 2026. Method: a transparency assessment that maps to the CoST IDS, political-economy analysis, and net present value, developed by CIPE with the NPV toolkit led by IEA-Kenya.
