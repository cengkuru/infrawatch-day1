The pattern

A sovereign loan that builds a railway is the same instrument everywhere. But Kenya logs it through the public-debt register while Ghana captures it mainly when a foreign entity holds an ownership stake. Same railway, in one dataset and invisible in the next. The fix is not to harmonise the laws, it is one shared, OC4IDS-anchored definition every country can map up to.
