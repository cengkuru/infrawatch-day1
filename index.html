Drag the purple dots. Keyboard: Tab to a dot, then arrow keys.

Most people draw a climb. The real line is nearly flat.

The gap you just drew is the intuition almost everyone shares: more transparency, better value. But the real line barely rises. The apparent link was mostly who funded the project, not transparency itself. Transparency does not make a project good. It lets you find out whether it is. That is what these three days take apart.
