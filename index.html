One question, walked through.

These are not charts with captions. Each one is a short visual essay: a single question, taken apart in front of you, where the picture and the point never disagree. Built to be understood in the room and to survive being shared after it.

What to expect from these visuals

One question each

Every piece answers a single question. No dashboards, no piles of metrics. If it cannot be said in one line, it is not on the page.

The picture argues the point

The visual is chosen so its first reading is the finding. You should not need a paragraph telling you not to misread it.

You are walked, not quizzed

Scroll and the visual moves with you, zooming to whatever the text is talking about. The work of reading the chart is done for you.

Honest about its limits

Where the evidence is thin, the piece says so on the chart itself, so the caveat travels even in a screenshot.
