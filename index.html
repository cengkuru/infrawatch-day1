InfraWatch Nairobi · a reusable method, not a workshop recap

InfraWatch does not prove publication makes a project good. It proves publication makes value testable, and gives the public standing to challenge the terms.

One method, three moves: see what is being funded and by whom, test whether its value can be judged, then surface the public voice that contract publication unlocks. The same three moves run on any foreign-funded project on the watch list.

Three moves, one library. Each piece answers one question and carries its own evidence status, so you can see at a glance what is ready to share and what still needs a check.
