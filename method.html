The method, at a glance

First you see it, then you test it, then the public can voice it.

Three moves to read any foreign-funded project. Each move is one question. Open a card to walk it in full.

Move 1 · See itDay 1 · 2 Jun

See it

What is being funded, and by whom?

Name the project and its funding type. The financing tells you which record to demand first.

name it→pull the IDS fields→flag the blind spot

Move 2 · Test itDay 2 · 3 Jun

Test it

Can its value be judged at all?

Score disclosure, then ask the prior question: were the terms published? If not, no verdict is possible, and that is the finding.

score→assess→split by funder
walk this move →
Move 3 · Voice itDay 3 · 4 Jun

Voice it

Who decides if it was worth it?

Aim the disclosure ask at who actually holds the terms, the foreign funder and the contractor, so the affected public gains standing.

find holders→ask→reach the public
