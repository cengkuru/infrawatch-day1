The blind spot is built into the money.

How a project is paid for decides what you can and cannot see. Each kind of money carries a predictable dark patch, shown as a black cell. The useful part: there is already one record that names what is hidden.

Sovereign loan

Hides the real debt.

dark: hidden debtclears it: loan terms

The headline is the project; the liability to the treasury sits out of view. Publish the loan terms and the guarantee, and the real debt appears.

Equity stake

Hides who really owns it.

dark: hidden ownershipclears it: beneficial ownership

An ownership stake can obscure who captures the value. Publish the beneficial owners and you see who is really behind it.

Concession / PPP

Hides the future bill.

dark: hidden future billclears it: contract terms

The long-term payments and guarantees can be invisible at the start. Publish the contract and the future bill becomes visible.

EPC + finance

Hides the mark-up.

dark: hidden mark-upclears it: cost breakdown

A bundled build-and-finance package can bury the price. Publish the cost breakdown and the price can be benchmarked.

Grant / aid

Hides the conditions.

dark: hidden conditionsclears it: source & conditions

Tied conditions and strings attached can stay out of sight. Publish the source and its conditions and the real terms show.

The finding, in one breath

Different money, different blind spot, but every blind spot has a record that already names it. Where the money comes from is a shortcut: it tells you, before you read a single document, which fact to ask them to publish.
