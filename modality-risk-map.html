The financing type predicted the blind spot every time.

Different countries, different financing routes, one diagnostic rule: the money type tells you which record to ask for first. A comparison is worthless without a narrative, and a narrative is worthless until the data is humanized, that was this room's doctrine. Here is the field tool it produces.
