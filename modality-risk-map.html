The blind spot is built into the money.

How a project is financed decides what you can and cannot see about it. Each funding type carries a predictable dark patch. The useful part: a single CoST IDS field already names what is hidden, so you know what to ask for.

Sovereign loan

Hides the real debt.

goes dark: hidden debtclears it: loan terms

The headline is the project; the liability to the treasury can sit out of view. Publish the loan terms and the guarantee, and the real debt appears.

Equity stake

Hides who really owns it.

goes dark: hidden ownershipclears it: beneficial ownership

An ownership stake can obscure who actually captures the value. Publish the beneficial owners and you see who is really behind it.

Concession / PPP

Hides the future bill.

goes dark: hidden future billclears it: contract terms

The long-term obligation, the payments and guarantees owed for years, can be invisible at the start. Publish the contract and the future bill becomes visible.

EPC + finance

Hides the mark-up.

goes dark: hidden mark-upclears it: cost breakdown

A bundled build-and-finance package can bury the price. Publish the cost breakdown and the price can be benchmarked.

Grant / aid

Hides the conditions.

goes dark: hidden conditionsclears it: source & conditions

Tied conditions and the strings attached can stay out of sight. Publish the source and its conditions and the real terms show.

The Day 1 finding, in one breath

Different money, different blind spot, but every blind spot has a field that already names it. The funding type is a shortcut: it tells you, before you read a single document, which disclosure field to demand.
