The financing type predicted the blind spot every time.

Different countries, different financing routes, one diagnostic rule: the money type tells you which record to ask for first. The lesson behind it: a comparison is worthless without a narrative, and the narrative only lands when the data becomes human. Here is the field tool it produces.
