Day 2 · reading a project by its money

Same outside.
Different secret.

Two projects can look identical and hide completely different things, because they were financed in different ways. One funding structure, one blind spot, one record to demand. Step through five.

The point, in one breath

A funding structure is a prediction engine. It tells you which fact will disappear before you read a single document, and the one record that brings it back into the light.

→The disclosure triage card

The whole diagnostic on one card. See the funding structure, ask for the record that reveals its blind spot. Screenshot it and take it into a real request.

The records, in CoST IDS terms. Loan terms and guarantee (Preparation, Implementation); beneficial ownership (Identification); contract terms and fiscal commitments (Procurement); cost estimate and price (Preparation, Procurement); funding source and conditions (Preparation).

The point. Where the money comes from predicts the blind spot, and the CoST IDS already contains the record that reveals it. A request to publish can be aimed precisely at the one fact the financing was always going to hide.
