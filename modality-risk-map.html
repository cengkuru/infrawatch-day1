What the scanner proves

A funding structure is a prediction engine. It tells you which fact will disappear before you read a single document, and which one record to demand first. Ask for the wrong thing and the blind spot stays dark.

The records, in CoST IDS terms. Loan terms and guarantee (Preparation, Implementation); beneficial ownership (Identification); contract terms and fiscal commitments (Procurement); cost estimate and price (Preparation, Procurement); funding source and conditions (Preparation).

The point. Where the money comes from predicts the blind spot, and the CoST IDS already contains the record that reveals it. A request to publish can be aimed precisely at the one fact the financing was always going to hide.
