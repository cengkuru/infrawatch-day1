Five projects. Five secrets.

The doctrine this room named, in its own words

A comparison is worthless without a narrative, and a narrative is worthless until the data is humanized. A funding structure is a prediction engine: it tells you which fact will disappear, and the one record that brings it back. The number only matters once a citizen can feel it.

The point. Where the money comes from predicts the blind spot, and the CoST IDS already contains the record that reveals it. A request to publish can be aimed precisely at the one fact the financing was always going to hide.
