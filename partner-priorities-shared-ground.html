Five members, three questions, one shared problem.

We asked each member the same three things, the problem they want to tackle, their role in it, and what success looks like. Their answers sounded different. Then the words started to line up.

The one shared problem

Money is committed before anyone can judge it.

Spend is locked in before the evidence is public, and the public cannot hold delivery to account afterwards. Every member's problem is a face of this one.

The key finding

We are more alike than we are different.

Five members arrived with what felt like five agendas. Underneath, one shared problem, and the same family of solutions: publish the evidence before the spend, and let the public follow it after. The coalition was already there. It just had to be found, not declared.
