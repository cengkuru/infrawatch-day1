Partner validation · confirming the facts before the method relies on them proposed, for CIPE confirmation

Validation means confirming or correcting each recorded fact, with a source

Partners are the authority on their own country and project facts. Validation is the pass where a partner goes through what the mapping has recorded about their projects and either confirms each fact or corrects it with the source that proves the correction. It travels through the validation sheet that accompanies the partner summary, and it is how factual corrections, never silent edits, enter the record.

Silence is not validation. An unreturned sheet leaves the recorded facts standing as unconfirmed, and they will be used with that status visible. Confirming is as informative as correcting; only a returned sheet moves a fact from recorded to validated.

What a completed validation looks like

Every row in the sheet carries one of three marks: confirmed; corrected, with the correcting fact and its source; or cannot confirm, with the reason. A sheet with unmarked rows is a partial return, not a completed validation. Corrections are confirmed by partners against their own sources, never substituted by the research team, and the return date travels with the message that delivers the sheet.

If you disagree with a classification

Facts are corrected; classifications are referred. A wrong date, financier or source is a factual correction: return it on the sheet with its evidence and it is applied. A disagreement about how a rule was applied, whether the project is a PPP, whether a source is government publication, whether a case is inside the definition, goes through referral and resolution: the evidence and both readings are recorded, CIPE decides, and the ruling becomes precedent for every partner's matching case. Partners verify facts; CoST recommends the method; CIPE approves programme decisions.
