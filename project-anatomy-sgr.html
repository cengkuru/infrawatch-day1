PROJECT FILE: NAIROBI SGR

illustrative dossier · not the audited record

1. What is it?

A railway, Mombasa to Nairobi. Stations, trains, ridership. Announced, opened, photographed.

2. How is it paid for?

A sovereign loan. A foreign export-import bank lends; the state borrows and guarantees.

3. Who decides?

Treasury, the railway, the lender. The high-power actors and the people who carry the cost barely overlap.

The Day 1 finding, in one breath

Transparency is not missing at random. It runs out exactly where it matters most. The questions you can answer about the SGR are the easy ones. The two that decide whether it was a good idea, what it cost and whether it was worth it, are the two stamped confidential.
