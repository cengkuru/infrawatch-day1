Project roles · who is who in a project's record

A project has five roles, and one role is never evidence of another

Every party in a project is recorded against the role it actually plays: who answers to the public, whose money is at risk, who was paid to build, who runs the asset, and who ultimately gains. One entity can hold several roles at once; what it is called, who owns it, and what flag it flies never substitute for what it does.

The trap: reading one role as proof of another. A foreign builder is not a foreign financier. A state-owned contractor is not the public authority. A project company on the contract is not the ultimate owner. Every misclassification in this bank's worked examples comes from collapsing two of these roles into one.

The five roles

RoleThe question it answersWhere the rules live
Responsible public authorityWho answers to the public for the project and its record? The only entity whose disclosure is scored.Responsible public authority
FinancierWhose money is at risk or owed for the asset? Decides entry and classification.Foreign financing · classification
BuilderWho was paid to construct it? Recorded, never decisive for entry or classification.Foreign financing (what does not count)
OperatorWho runs the asset or service, and carries operating decisions and risk? Central to the PPP tests and SOE role classification.PPP · SOE
Beneficial ownerWho ultimately gains from a company involved, traced through holding structures?Beneficial ownership

The confusions this map settles

Looks likeActually
Foreign builder, so foreign-financedBuilding is revenue, not money at risk. Only the financier role decides entry.
State-owned enterprise, so public authorityAn SOE acting as contractor or concessionaire sits on the private side of the tests; the authority is whoever answers to the public.
The SPV signed everything, so it is the ownerThe SPV is a party recorded by role; the contracting authority stays the project owner, and the SPV's ultimate owners are recorded separately.
The lender is the PPP private partnerA passive lender fails the management test; financing alone does not make a partner.
Registered in Singapore, so SingaporeanRegistration is a vehicle; the role is attributed to the ultimate owner, traced to origin.

What recording by role changes

Each role feeds a different decision. The financier decides entry and classification; the authority decides whose record is scored; the operator and risk-carrier decide the PPP outcome; the beneficial owner feeds the PPP disclosure questions; all of them land in the attribution record. Record each party against each role it holds, and the hard cases resolve one role at a time.
