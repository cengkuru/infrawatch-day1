Referral and resolution · when the rule does not settle it proposed, for CIPE confirmation

Do not force a score or classification the evidence does not settle

Most points resolve by rule. When one does not, the assessment has exactly four honest states, and choosing the right one is what keeps twenty country teams comparable. Improvised local exceptions are how comparability dies quietly.

Never invent an exception to make a hard case fit. A stretched definition or a quietly forced score corrupts every comparison built on it. The referral route exists precisely so a hard case can stay hard until it is ruled on.

The four situations, and the action each one takes

SituationAction
Evidence is missingContinue the sufficient search; if the search completes with nothing and the point is due, record it by its evidence state. Missing evidence is never a referral.
Evidence is present and the rule is clearApply the rule and record the source. Most points end here.
Evidence is present but the classification is genuinely ambiguousFlag the project or point and refer it for a CIPE decision, with the evidence attached and both readings stated. Score everything the ambiguity does not touch; hold only what it does.
Sources conflictKeep both figures, credit the disclosure, flag the divergence per the conflicting-figures rule. A conflict is a finding, not a deadlock.

What a referral contains

A referral is a decision request, not a complaint: the project and point, the evidence found with sources, the two (or more) plausible readings, which rule fails to settle them, and what the team did in the meantime. It enters the database as an exception with its rationale, so the pending state is visible, never silent.

What happens while the ruling is pending

The assessment does not stop. The flagged item is held from any result it would change; everything else on the project is scored normally. When the ruling lands, it is applied, recorded, and becomes precedent: the same answer then applies to every partner's matching case, which is the point of referring instead of improvising. CIPE holds final methodology and scoring authority; partners own their evidence and their country corrections.
