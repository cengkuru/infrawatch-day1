Kenya SGR Publication Test

This page shows how one infrastructure project is tested against the Project Publication Test. It asks what is already public, what is still needed, and what a partner can request next.

This page does not judge whether the SGR was good or bad. It tests whether the public records are enough to judge its value.

Move 1 · See it

What is the project, and who funds it?

A sovereign loan from the Export-Import Bank of China to the Government of Kenya, for the railway built by Kenya Railways.1 funder identified

Why this matters

The financing type tells us which records are needed before value can be tested. A sovereign loan carries the debt and any guarantees in the loan agreement, not in the announcement.

Move 2 · Test it

Can value for money be judged from public records?

Only partly, and only after the fact. The loan agreements were confidential for years, then partly released in November 2022, but the disclosure was widely judged incomplete, with collateral and key terms still questioned.2 disclosed late and in part

Why this matters

The loan drove Kenya's public debt sharply higher, from about 38% of GDP in 2012 toward roughly 70% a decade later, and the railway has run at a loss.3 Whether the project was worth that cost could not be judged up front, because the terms were not public when the money was committed. Late, partial disclosure is the gap this test is built to surface.

Move 3 · Voice it

Who needs the information, and where might it sit?

The affected public, route communities and taxpayers. The records most likely sit with the lender and the builder, not only the government. holders identified

Why this matters

A request for the information has to reach whoever actually holds it. If it only goes to the state, the records that decide the question may never surface.

The request for project information

Publish, or confirm where to review, the loan agreement, repayment schedule, and appraisal assumptions.

These are the records needed to assess whether the project offered value for money. This maps to the CoST publication fields for loan terms and guarantees. It is a request for information, not a finding of wrongdoing.

What a partner does next

Before circulating this anywhere, verify the figures against the source record, confirm which of these documents are already public, and choose the institution best placed to request the rest. IEA-Kenya is the natural lead as the anchor partner.
