Worked example · one project, run end to end

The Kenya SGR, run through the InfraWatch method.

This is the proof: one real foreign-funded project taken through see, test, voice, producing an evidence checklist, the publication ask that matters, a finding row, and a partner action note. The figures are as reported across this resource, to be confirmed against loan agreements before external use.

Standard Gauge Railway · Kenya · partner: IEA-Kenya · status: as-reported, figures to confirm

Move 1 · See it

What is being funded, and by whom

Project
Standard Gauge Railway (SGR), Kenya, Mombasa–Nairobi corridor
Funder
Chinese state lenders (Exim Bank of China)
Financing type
Sovereign loan
What this type hides
the real debt and contingent liability, in the loan agreement, not the press release
Move 2 · Test it

Can its value be judged

Terms published
Partly. The sovereign guarantee is known; the full loan terms are confidential.
Value (NPV)
Judged only from outcomes, not up front. Loan ~5% of GDP, debt-service strain on Kenya Railways; no full NPV possible without the terms.
Verdict basis
Outcome-based, as reported. The point is not the announced cost; it is the debt-service exposure and contingent liability that stayed in the agreement.
Move 3 · Voice it

Who needs standing to challenge the terms

Affected
Communities along the corridor, and Kenyan taxpayers carrying the debt service
Holds the terms
The foreign funder (lender confidentiality) and the EPC contractor, not only the state
Output 1

Evidence checklist

  • Funder identified (Exim Bank of China)
  • Financing type classified (sovereign loan)
  • Terms published (partly; full loan terms withheld)
  • Value assessable up front (only from outcomes)
  • Affected public identified
  • Holder of terms identified (funder + contractor)
Output 2

The publication ask that matters most

Publish the loan agreement and the sovereign guarantee. A sovereign loan hides the real debt and contingent liability. In CoST IDS terms: loan terms + guarantee. Until these are published, no minister, auditor, or citizen can independently judge whether the SGR was worth its debt-service cost.
Output 3

Finding row

SGR, Kenya · Exim Bank of China · sovereign loan. Terms partly published; value judged only from outcomes, not up front. as-reported, figures to confirm

Output 4

Partner action note

Take to the foreign funder and the EPC contractor: a request to publish the loan agreement and the sovereign guarantee, so that corridor communities and Kenyan taxpayers gain the standing to challenge the debt-service terms. Route via IEA-Kenya as the anchor partner. Confirm figures against the loan agreement before external circulation.
