What the assessed responses change

Sri Lanka · Verité Research

Operational detail strengthens the working method

CoST working position

  • Fair-zero, the funder-only ceiling and recency remain supported.
  • Minimum records, four applicability states and source provenance need field-level guidance.
  • Projects remain the primary unit, with phases and contracting processes nested beneath.
  • No selected Sri Lankan project is a PPP, supporting an explicit no-PPP route.
Ethiopia · Stalwart

Most tested positions work, with one scoring ambiguity

Assessed

  • Four of six core rules and all four CoST recommendations were marked as working.
  • Modjo-Hawassa Expressway lots 1 to 4 support contract-level reporting within one project.
  • None of the 20 selected projects meets the proposed PPP definition.
  • The ceiling of 2 for non-government sources is clear; the distinction between scores 1 and 2 is not.

Feedback and current treatment

Open each item to see the consequence, evidence boundary and next decision. These are not partner rankings.

🇱🇰 🇪🇹Keep the fair-zero architectureAssessed

Feedback: both pilot responses support distinguishing a true disclosure failure from information that is not yet due, does not apply or has not been sufficiently searched.

Treatment: retain the architecture and shorten the field explanation with a decision path and worked example. The report's headline diagnostic figures do not change.

🇱🇰 🇪🇹Add a no-selected-PPP routeAssessed

Feedback: neither Sri Lanka nor Ethiopia has a PPP in the selected sample.

Treatment: propose a form route that records this finding and bypasses the PPP-only collection trial. This remains a CoST working recommendation until CIPE confirms the cohort-wide form change.

🇪🇹Define scores 1 and 2 for non-government evidenceCIPE decision pending

Feedback: Ethiopia asked why a detailed funder report and a single-line media mention could both sit under the same ceiling of 2.

Treatment: draft one sentence using accessibility and completeness to distinguish scores 1 and 2, while recording provenance and reliability separately. The exact rule is not yet CIPE-confirmed.

🇱🇰 🇪🇹Use contract-level profiles within one projectStrengthened

Feedback: Sri Lanka supplied programme and contracting-process cases; Ethiopia named Modjo-Hawassa Expressway lots 1 to 4.

Treatment: keep the project as the primary monitoring unit and record awarded contracts separately. A blended project figure still requires a defensible aggregation rule.

🇪🇹Keep Ethiopia project classifications openOpen evidence gap

Observed boundary: Ethiopia's project-correction table was blank and the no-corrections box was not marked. The PPP collection trial was not completed beyond an n/a entry.

Treatment: do not call the Ethiopian project register partner-validated. Direct project evidence or an explicit partner confirmation is still needed. A newer Ethiopia sheet dated 13 August is in the shared folder and has not yet been assessed; it may close this gap.

🇱🇰Hold thresholds and selection targets for comparisonCIPE decision pending

Feedback: Verité proposed a presumptive 10 percent foreign-financing screen and a two-projects-per-funder target where the eligible universe permits.

Treatment: keep both as candidates. Compare them with additional country responses before any cohort-wide decision.

Who decides what

Partners verify country and project facts. CoST recommends and drafts the method. CIPE confirms programme-wide decisions. No item moves to “working draft,” “CIPE confirmed” or “published” without evidence of that exact state.
