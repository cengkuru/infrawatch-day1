Three wording decisions close Friday Due Friday 21 August

CIPE confirms all three. Each is programme-wide wording, not a country call. Nothing else on this page is time-bound.

  1. Does post-construction foreign equity qualify under R1? Working recommendation: yes, where the equity is material, labelled as an ownership change. Raised by Indonesia.
  2. When is a concession still ongoing for recency? Working recommendation: while in force, with recency running from award or last material amendment. Raised by Indonesia and Malaysia.
  3. What is the public-function test for GLC and strategic assets? Working recommendation: public asset or public service with material foreign finance qualifies; purely private commercial does not. Raised by Malaysia.

If Friday passes with no confirmation, each working recommendation above applies provisionally, is marked unconfirmed on this page, and is re-opened at the next CIPE review rather than treated as settled. Named CIPE confirmer and channel: to be filled in by CoST before this page is circulated.

Everything else proceeds on the nine filed sheets, assessed against the four rule-scanned so far. The core method holds, and a later Cambodia or Nigeria sheet enters as a versioned delta rather than a reopening.

Feedback and current treatment

Ordered by what is owed and when, not by theme. Open each item to see the feedback, the current treatment and the evidence boundary. These are not partner rankings.

Closes Friday 21 August · 3 · CIPE confirms

🇮🇩Say whether post-construction foreign equity qualifiesDue Friday

Feedback: Indonesia's two Trans Java toll roads received foreign equity after construction, through an INA acquisition and later APG and ADIA co-investment. R1 does not say whether secondary-market equity is part of the project's financing or financial security.

Treatment: working recommendation is yes, if the equity is material, and label it as an ownership change rather than construction finance. Needs CIPE confirmation before Friday.

🇮🇩 🇲🇾Treat a live concession as ongoing for recencyDue Friday

Feedback: Kanci-Pejagan opened in 2010 and Pejagan-Pemalang in 2018, but both remain live concessions. Malaysia separately retained a 2015 Japanese water tunnel after finding no stronger recent alternative.

Treatment: a concession is ongoing while it remains in force. Recency runs from award or the last material amendment. The aged-project exception stays available when documented.

🇲🇾Define the public-function test for GLC and strategic assetsDue Friday

Feedback: IDEAS asks to keep the PETRONAS Biorefinery, Rosmari-Marjoram gas development and the Cainiao airport logistics hub. Each sits on the line between public infrastructure and private commercial activity.

Treatment: qualify a project when it is a public asset or public service with material foreign finance. Exclude a purely private commercial facility where government is only the regulator. Named Malaysian cases stay country decisions until that wording is confirmed.

Open, no fixed date · 6 · CIPE decision or CoST drafting

🇪🇹Define scores 1 and 2 for non-government evidenceCIPE decision

Feedback: Ethiopia asked why a detailed funder report and a single-line media mention could both sit under the same ceiling of 2.

Treatment: draft one sentence using accessibility and completeness to distinguish scores 1 and 2, while recording provenance and reliability separately. The exact rule is not yet CIPE-confirmed.

🇪🇹Set the sufficient-search bar for hard-to-find public recordsCIPE decision

Feedback: not everything reaches journalists or easily accessible reports. Information can be public while finding it requires knowing where to look or specialized technical skills; access may be limited by the nature of infrastructure work rather than by the government.

Treatment: define the minimum search steps that must be completed before an unsearched cell can become a zero, so a hard search and a failed disclosure are never conflated. This extends the fair-zero decision path already in drafting.

🇱🇰Hold thresholds and selection targets for comparisonCIPE decision

Feedback: Verité proposed a presumptive 10 percent foreign-financing screen and a two-projects-per-funder target where the eligible universe permits.

Treatment: keep both as candidates. Compare them with additional country responses before any cohort-wide decision.

🇪🇹Name the point at which a cohort is completeCoST to draft

Feedback: a comment on the partner summary shows two instructions in tension: replace projects that later fail eligibility from the buffer list, yet once a cohort is completed, selections are not reopened. Partners cannot tell whether a late eligibility problem can still be fixed.

Treatment: name the completion point explicitly, with validation sign-off as the working proposal, so buffer substitutions stay open until then and stop after. Wording is a CoST drafting fix; the completion point itself needs CIPE confirmation.

🇪🇹 🇱🇰State the PPP module as one threshold plus four evidence familiesCoST to draft

Feedback: the summary refers to three functional tests that Section 6 never names; it actually describes a single threshold (does the contract show the required PPP characteristics) plus a four-family Tier 1 review. Verité separately confirmed the reading that ordinary projects carry 40 indicators and confirmed PPPs carry 40 plus 4, and asked how the funding-sources family fits with core indicator 12, which already captures funding sources for every project.

Treatment: name and define the threshold and the four families explicitly, confirm the 40 plus 4 structure, and state what the Tier 1 funding-sources questions add beyond indicator 12 so nothing is double-counted.

🇪🇹Keep Ethiopia project classifications openEvidence gap · CoST action

Treatment: do not call the Ethiopian project register partner-validated. Direct project evidence or an explicit partner confirmation is still needed.

Owned next step: a newer Ethiopia sheet dated 13 August is filed and unassessed. Assessing it is a CoST task, not a CIPE decision, and it may close this gap outright. Owner and date: to be assigned by CoST. This is the only evidence gap on the page; the other open items are wording decisions.
